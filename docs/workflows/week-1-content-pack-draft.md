## Post 1 — A website should answer the customer's next question

**Suggested timing:** Early week  
**Channel:** Facebook; adapt for LinkedIn  
**Format:** Text post with a simple branded graphic  
**Objective:** Educate business owners about useful website content

**Draft copy**

A business website should do more than look good.

When someone visits, they should quickly understand:
- What you offer
- Who you serve
- Where to find you or how to contact you
- What they should do next

If customers have to search through several pages just to find the basics, they may leave before contacting you.

At Tambala Technologies, we build websites and digital solutions around what a business needs its customers to understand and do.

What is the one thing you wish more customers could find easily about your business?

**Visual direction:** A clean, original mock-up showing a business homepage with clearly labelled services, location/contact information, and one visible call to action. Do not use a real client's branding or imply that the mock-up belongs to a client.

**Claim check:** General educational advice; no performance result is claimed.  
**CTA:** Invite business owners to comment or message Tambala to discuss their website needs.

## Post 2 — Design for the phone in your customer's hand

**Suggested timing:** Midweek  
**Channel:** Facebook; adapt for LinkedIn  
**Format:** Before/after-style illustrative graphic, clearly labelled as a concept  
**Objective:** Demonstrate awareness of mobile usability

**Draft copy**

Many people will first encounter a business while browsing on a phone.

That makes the mobile experience important:
- Text should be easy to read.
- Contact details should be easy to find.
- Buttons should be comfortable to tap.
- Pages should be organized so visitors do not have to guess where to go.

A website should help customers take the next step—not make them work to find it.

Tambala Technologies provides web development and digital product development services for businesses looking to improve how they present themselves online.

If your website is difficult to use on a phone, it may be worth reviewing the experience from your customer's point of view.

**Visual direction:** Show a generic illustrative mobile homepage with readable text and a clear contact button. Avoid claiming a measured speed or conversion improvement.

**Claim check:** No numerical performance claim or guaranteed result.  
**CTA:** Invite businesses to ask about a website review or discuss a future project; do not promise a free audit unless the Founder approves that offer.  
**Status:** Pending review.
