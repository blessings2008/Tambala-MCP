# Media & Content Production — Weekly Content Workflow

**Department:** Media & Content Production  
**Marketing owner:** Marketing & Brand Management  
**Status:** Draft workflow; content remains unpublished until approved.

## Purpose

Turn approved Tambala marketing priorities into a practical, consistent content pack that supports credibility and qualified sales conversations without inventing claims or publishing automatically.

## Handoff

1. **Marketing & Brand Management** supplies the objective, audience, campaign theme, channel, desired action, and any approved campaign constraints.
2. **Media & Content Production** develops the calendar, copy, and asset requirements.
3. **Central Research & Intelligence** verifies external facts and checks claims when research is needed.
4. **Marketing & Brand Management** reviews brand consistency and commercial relevance.
5. **Founder** approves public-facing claims, final copy, and any publication or scheduling action.

## Weekly content pack

Every proposed post must include:

- Proposed date and channel
- Content objective and target audience
- Content format (text, carousel, short video, case study, etc.)
- Complete draft copy
- Visual or asset requirements
- Verified source material for factual claims
- Call to action
- Approval status: draft, needs evidence, pending Founder approval, approved, or published
- Publication record only after the post is actually published

## Suitable initial content themes

- Practical website and digital-presence advice for Malawian businesses.
- Short demonstrations of Tambala's verified web-development and software-development services.
- Behind-the-scenes explanations of how a website project is planned and improved.
- Portfolio case studies only when the project facts are accurate and permission to showcase the work has been obtained.
- Educational explainers about business websites, usability, mobile responsiveness, and online credibility.
- Product updates only when the relevant product status and features are verified and disclosure is appropriate.

## Content safeguards

- Do not invent testimonials, customer quotes, results, client counts, revenue outcomes, partnerships, awards, or guarantees.
- Do not present demos or staging projects as completed live client deployments.
- Do not publish client photos, logos, private correspondence, or project details without permission where needed.
- Do not make claims about payment integrations, security certifications, booking systems, or other features unless verified.
- Do not schedule or publish externally without explicit approval.
- Reuse approved brand assets and Tambala identity consistently; do not create a repetitive generic template for every post.

## Acceptance test

A weekly content pack is ready for review when it includes a coherent set of dated ideas, complete draft copy, clear asset requirements, verified claims, useful calls to action, and a visible approval state. It is not considered published until publication is confirmed.
